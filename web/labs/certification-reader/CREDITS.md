# Certification reading

The main settings entry displays actual first- and fifth-chapter unissued art
specimens. It does not issue an award or create a score. The rejected museum,
furniture, room textures, carrying animation and room score are not included.

- First-chapter document, impression and artwork: chapter-scroll-v12.
- Fifth-chapter document and independently authored parchment: v14 / v13.
- Continuous paper and residual curvature: v6, using Paperlab (Apache-2.0 and
  NOTICE in chapter-scroll/vendor).
- Physical fastening geometry and path: v17; accepted finishing: v19.
- Regional atmosphere and interaction: v20, using Pavel Fluid and Ashima noise.
- Three.js and loaders: MIT. Source notices remain in the vendor directories and
  bundled viewer's license banner.
- Aboensis font: SIL Open Font License, chapter-scroll/assets.
- Viewer integration and lifecycle derive from the prior scroll-museum adapter;
  no museum scene is imported. First-chapter paper/record are its actual v12
  artifact; the common v19 physical engine includes its established paper folds.
- The display GLB only removes the old embedded images replaced by v19 finishing.
  Its geometry, skin, morph data and UVs remain unchanged. Derivation evidence is
  recorded in assets/keeper-display.json.
