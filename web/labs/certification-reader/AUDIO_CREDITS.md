# Certification reader — music and material recordings

Current revision: 2026-10-22. The product uses one background recording,
recorded leather/rolled-parchment textures and a quiet prong/frame contact.
The retired book-open/book-close/page-flip/placement/cloth/latch montage is not
used. Historical sources remain recoverable; they are not the current mix.

## Background music

**A New Town (RPG Theme)** by **The Cynic Project / cynicmusic**,
CC0 1.0: <https://opengameart.org/content/a-new-town-rpg-theme>.
Runtime: `assets/audio-music/a-new-town.mp3`, unchanged 766,291 bytes.
A complete recording with its natural end and a short rest; no seamless music
loop or historical-medieval authenticity is claimed.

## Parchment movement — attribution required

**Spell Scroll / Unrolling Parchment** by **spookymodem**, OpenGameArt,
**CC BY 3.0**: <https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/spell-scroll>.
License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>.
Derived runtime: `assets/audio-foley/parchment-roll-loop.wav`.
Changes: interval 0.54–2.75s, mono downmix, DC removal, a short same-recording
crossfade seam, soft transient compression and gain adjustment, 16-bit PCM.
Runtime low-pass, level and restrained playback rate follow paper winding.
This recording and its derivative are distributed with the above attribution
and CC BY 3.0 license. No creator endorsement is implied.

## Leather movement

**Leather Stretched** by **altfuture**, Freesound, CC0:
<https://freesound.org/people/altfuture/sounds/174638/>.
The archived download is the author's publicly accessible **HQ MP3 preview**,
not the original 96kHz/24-bit WAV. No login restriction was bypassed.
Derived runtime: `assets/audio-foley/leather-flex-loop.wav`.
Changes: browser decode, mono downmix, interval14.7–16.1s, DC removal, a short
same-recording crossfade seam, soft transient compression, gain and PCM export.
Actual leather bend, release, feed and slide coordinates drive its level.

## Metal contact

**RPG Audio** by **Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)**, CC0:
<https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio>.
Runtime: the existing `assets/audio-foley/metalClick.wav`, unchanged PCM conversion.
Only a quiet touch when the actual rigid prong parks/seats against its frame;
no invented mechanical latch or random impact chain.

## Traceability and listening limits

Complete author/source/license/change notice, original files and exact derivative
parameters: [material-recordings/CREDITS.md](assets/audio-sources/material-recordings/CREDITS.md),
[derivatives.json](assets/audio-sources/material-recordings/derivatives.json),
and the current [audio manifest](assets/audio-sources/manifest.json).
The corresponding original author pages and signal analysis are archived in
`research/scroll-reader-correction-2026-10-22/audio-sources/` in the repository.
The former mix's manifest is retained as `assets/audio-sources/legacy-manifest-2026-10-21.json`.

Music and material audio default on after the real user tap. Browsers may still
require another gesture to resume; loading failure never blocks the artwork.
Motion/decoding tests are not human timbre or iPhone-speaker listening acceptance.
No new purchase, subscription or recurring generation cost was added.
