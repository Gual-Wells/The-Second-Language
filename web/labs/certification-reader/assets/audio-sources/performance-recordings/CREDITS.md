# Recorded gesture sources — v41

All three sources are author-released CC0 1.0. Original public files, decoded
PCM and exact cuts remain alongside this file; sources.json records URLs and
hashes. No subscription, login bypass or paid generation was used.

## Parchment - Unroll — Vrymaa

<https://freesound.org/people/Vrymaa/sounds/753282/>
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>

The author performed old, stiff parchment unrolling with a **dried bamboo
leaf** for museum-restoration scenes. Substitute-material foley, not a real
archaeological parchment recording. We obtained the public HQ MP3 derivative,
not the login-protected WAV. 48 kHz PCM decoding restores no MP3 information.
paper-open-v41.wav: .46–3.50s; paper-close-v41.wav: 13.10–16.40s, a different
forward performance used as a roll-in surrogate, not an actual recorded
closing action. No reversal.

## Leather Belt Stretching 1_8 — Joao_Janz

<https://freesound.org/people/Joao_Janz/sounds/493154/>
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>

Author: leather belt recorded on Tascam DR-40, gain staging, gentle high/low
filtering and denoising. Public HQ MP3 derivative obtained, not original
24-bit WAV. leather-release-v41.wav: .20–2.38s; bending/withdrawal, not a latch.

## Belt buckle — Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank

<https://bigsoundbank.com/belt-buckle-s0575.html>
<https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html>

Original WAV: 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit; leather belt buckle with some handling
noise. buckle-park-v41.wav: .47–.79s; buckle-seat-v41.wav: 3.69–4.07s.
Restrained prong/frame contacts, not a spring lock.

## Processing and limits

Fixed contiguous selection, mono decode, DC removal, broad frequency cleanup,
static material-specific gain and 12/35 ms edge fades. No tanh/limiting
compression, RMS leveling, looping, reversal or pitch modulation.
derivatives.json and scripts/build-scroll-performances.py retain exact
parameters. Source metadata, licensing and technical playback checks do not
constitute human listening approval. Candidate for user audition.
