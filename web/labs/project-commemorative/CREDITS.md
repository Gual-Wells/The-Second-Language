# The Returning Thread

An original contemporary private commemorative work for Gual Wells and The
Second Language. It is not a historical document, assessment award, or record
of the project's founding date.

The English verse, Chinese translation, composition and paper direction were
created for this work. Creative dispatch and independent art review used the
requested gpt-6.1-sol / xhigh configuration. The paper and miniature were made
with the built-in imagegen tool; its underlying image model and quality
parameter are not exposed. No museum image was copied into the artifact.

Material references (research, not pixel sources):

- [Morgan Library: manuscript damage and repairs](https://www.themorgan.org/blog/holey-moly-historical-damage-and-repairs-medieval-manuscripts)
- [Bodleian: material manuscript study](https://hab.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/en/blog/blog-post-26/)
- [Getty: the medieval scriptorium](https://www.getty.edu/art/exhibitions/scriptorium/)

Original image masters and selected edit prompt are retained in
`research/project-commemorative-2026-10-21/`. The six once-typeset PNG layers
are the display originals. Their byte digests are in `assets/surfaces.json`;
the canonical content digest includes text, making record and source assets,
and excludes publication time and the PNGs that print the digest.

`../chapter-scroll/assets/`. Shared paper, keeper, finishing, atmosphere,

and [audio credits](../certification-reader/AUDIO_CREDITS.md).
