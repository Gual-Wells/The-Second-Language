# A river opens, but recovery takes longer

<!-- SENTENCE:P01S001 USE:U00407 -->
The Elwha River's two hydroelectric dams supplied alternative energy while blocking fish, sediment and wood from moving through the watershed.
`埃尔瓦河的两座水电坝在提供替代能源的同时，阻断了鱼类、泥沙和木材在流域中的移动。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S002 USE:U02601 -->
After decades of pressure from the Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe and its allies, removal between 2011 and 2014 reopened more than seventy miles of salmon habitat.
`经过下埃尔瓦克拉勒姆部落及其盟友数十年的推动，2011至2014年的拆坝工程重新开放了七十多英里的鲑鱼生境。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S003 USE:U01212 -->
A conservationist following this river therefore has to consider both a fish's passage and the material that makes a spawning ground.
`因此，关注这条河的自然保护工作者，既要考虑鱼的通道，也要考虑形成产卵场的物质。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S004 USE:U01207,U03109 -->
Conservation here involves reconnecting processes, rather than protecting one isolated, hard-to-reach pool while the rest of the system remains divided.
`这里的环境保护涉及重新连接各个过程，而不是只保护一处偏远、难以到达的水潭，却让其余水系继续分隔。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P01S005 USE:U03302 -->
Once the barriers were gone, salmon could migrate from the ocean into river reaches closed to them for a century.
`障碍消失后，鲑鱼便能从海洋迁入曾对它们封闭了一个世纪的河段。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S006 USE:U03306 -->
For a long-distance migrator such as steelhead, access to an upstream reach changes the possible journey, not merely the view at a dam.
`对于虹鳟这样的长距离迁徙动物，进入上游河段的通道改变的是它可能完成的旅程，而不仅仅是坝址的景象。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S007 USE:U03307,U03310 -->
Migratory fish connect places that a map divides into river, estuary and sea; each returning migrant carries marine nutrients inland.
`洄游鱼类把地图上分成河流、河口和海洋的地方连接起来；每一条返回的洄游鱼都把海洋营养带入内陆。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S008 USE:U02002,U03304 -->
A salmon that emerges from the water during a leap is visible for a moment, whereas much of its migration remains beneath the surface.
`跃出水面的鲑鱼只会短暂进入视野，而它迁徙过程中的大部分活动仍在水下进行。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S009 USE:U03305 -->
Its migrational route is also a route by which nutrients return to the river's food web.
`它的迁徙路线，也是营养重新进入河流食物网的一条路径。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P01S010 USE:U04402 -->
The water released another traveller: sediment that had accumulated behind the dams.
`河水还释放了另一类迁移者：此前在坝后积累的沉积物。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S011 -->
USGS researchers reported that more than ten million cubic metres eroded from the former reservoirs during the three years of removal and were transported into the lower river, delta and nearby coastal waters.
`美国地质调查局研究人员报告，在三年的拆坝期间，原水库中有一千多万立方米的沉积物被侵蚀，并被搬运到河流下游、三角洲及附近沿海水域。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S012 USE:U03303,U04403 -->
Water can make this material migrate downstream and then leave sedimented layers where its carrying conditions change.
`水可以使这些物质向下游迁移，并在搬运条件改变的地方留下沉积层。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S013 USE:U04407 -->
Sedimentation thus changes the channel and estuary through which the fish must travel.
`因此，沉积过程会改变鱼类必须经过的河道和河口。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S014 USE:U01225,U01228 -->
A conservative figure of ten million cubic metres already describes a disturbance extending well beyond the dam sites, even when its volume is assessed conservatively.
`即使对体积作保守评估，一千万立方米这一偏保守的数值，也已描述出一场远远延伸至坝址以外的扰动。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S015 USE:U04537 -->
Shifting deposits and channels make a single photograph an incomplete account of the river.
`不断移位的沉积物和河道，使一张照片无法完整呈现这条河的状况。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P01S016 USE:U00501,U00507 -->
Researchers could anticipate a sediment pulse, and collected baseline information in anticipation of the changes.
`研究人员能够预料到泥沙脉冲，并为预期中的变化事先收集了基线信息。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S017 USE:U00508 -->
That anticipation mattered because elevated suspended sediment depressed both the density and the diversity of bottom-dwelling invertebrates below the dams.
`这种预先考虑十分重要，因为升高的悬浮泥沙浓度，同时压低了坝址下游底栖无脊椎动物的密度和多样性。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S018 USE:U01701,U01704 -->
As turbidity diminished, rapidly reproducing generalists recovered quickly, but the diminution of the sediment disturbance did not restore every measure at the same speed.
`随着浑浊程度降低，繁殖迅速的广适性类群很快恢复，但泥沙扰动的减弱，并未使所有指标以同样速度恢复。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S019 USE:U01901,U03801 -->
NOAA reports that density rapidly rebounded while diversity initially remained low: a shortage of kinds can persist, and recovered numbers do not prove a diverse assemblage.
`美国国家海洋和大气管理局报告，密度迅速回升时，多样性起初仍然偏低：种类偏少的问题可能持续存在，数量恢复并不能证明已形成多样的生物组合。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S020 USE:U03805 -->
A persistent shortage of kinds makes the composition of the food web a separate question from the count of its members.
`种类长期偏少，使食物网由哪些类群组成，成为有别于其成员数量的另一个问题。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P01S021 USE:U03001 -->
The Tribe has continued to intervene through revegetation, restored floodplain connections and engineered logjams.
`部落继续通过恢复植被、恢复洪泛区连接以及建造人工木坝来进行干预。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S022 USE:U01211 -->
These conservational measures work on the conditions that fish encounter, rather than waiting for the removal itself to do everything.
`这些保护措施作用于鱼类所遇到的环境条件，而不是等待拆坝工程本身解决所有问题。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S023 USE:U00511,U03804 -->
In planning another removal, a useful anticipator would allow for the persistence of ecological effects after the last concrete barrier is gone.
`在规划另一次拆坝时，一个善于预先考虑的人，会把最后一道混凝土屏障消失后生态影响仍将持续的情况纳入考虑。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S024 USE:U03006,U00113 -->
Chance can still intervene, so an adaptive programme changes its next action when monitoring reveals a different response.
`偶然因素仍可能介入，因此，适应性项目会在监测显示不同反应时，调整下一步行动。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S025 USE:U04617,U04618,U03114 -->
Managers who aim to run a fishery sustainably cannot assess its sustainability in isolation from the river's food and sediment pathways.
`希望以生态可持续方式经营渔业的管理者，不能脱离河流的食物和泥沙路径来单独评价渔业的生态可持续性。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S026 USE:U04615,U03809 -->
Sustainable recovery requires persistently following these connections, not treating a rising fish count as the end of the work.
`可持续的恢复，需要持续追踪这些联系，而不是把鱼类数量上升视为工作的终点。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P01S027 USE:U05009,U04702,U04705 -->
Monitoring has already yielded tangible evidence of changed passage and food webs; the different recovery rates show tangibly why opening a river and recovering it are different achievements.
`监测已经提供了通行条件和食物网发生变化的明确证据；不同的恢复速度，具体显示出打通河流与恢复河流是两种不同的成果。`

# Keeping water, growing fruit

<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS001 USE:U01609,U00415,U03808 -->
Look at a cultivated apple tree before looking at its harvest: its alternate leaves do not grow as opposite pairs, and its persistent calyx remains on the fruit.
`在查看收成之前，先看一棵栽培苹果树：它的互生叶不成对对生，宿存花萼则保留在果实上。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS002 -->
The Canadian Food Inspection Agency describes apple trees as sensitive both to drought and to poor drainage.
`加拿大食品检验局指出，苹果树既对干旱敏感，也不耐排水不良。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS003 USE:U04221,U04217 -->
A soil's retentiveness matters, but soil that is retentive of moisture must still allow the roots a suitable environment.
`土壤的保水能力很重要，但能保持水分的土壤，仍必须为根系提供适宜的环境。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS004 USE:U01209,U01817 -->
Water conservation therefore means matching supply to the soil and the tree, rather than adding water indiscriminately.
`因此，节水意味着使供水适合土壤和树木，而不是不加区别地浇水。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS005 USE:U01612,U01230 -->
Imagine a small mixed garden in which a cultivator grows apples outside and starts tender plants in a conservatory.
`设想一个小型混合园圃：一位耕种者在室外种苹果，并在温室里培育不耐寒植物的幼苗。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS006 USE:U01601,U01607 -->
Before cultivating the land, the grower identifies which areas are cultivable and which remain waterlogged.
`耕种土地之前，种植者先确定哪些区域可以耕作，哪些区域仍然积水。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS007 USE:U01613,U01602 -->
A mechanical cultivator can cultivate the soil between suitable rows, loosening it instead of being driven indiscriminately against roots.
`机械耕耘机可以为合适行列之间的土壤松土，而不是不加区别地开到根部附近。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS008 USE:U01815,U01610 -->
Indiscriminate treatment of every patch as identical would ignore the very drainage differences on which cultivation depends.
`不加选择地把每块地都作同样处理，会忽略栽培所依赖的排水差异。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS009 USE:U00412 -->
A gardener who checks the soil on alternate days can record each observation without turning the log into an automatic watering schedule.
`园丁每隔一天检查一次土壤时，可以记录每次观察，而不必把日志变成自动浇水的时间表。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS010 USE:U01201,U01603 -->
Such observations help the gardener conserve water while continuing to cultivate fruit.
`这些观察有助于园丁在继续栽培水果的同时节约用水。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS011 USE:U01906 -->
A diversity of varieties spreads the questions beyond the largest apple: ripening time, flavour and local conditions also matter.
`多种不同品种，使选择不再只围绕最大的苹果展开：成熟时间、风味和当地条件同样重要。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS012 USE:U05016 -->
A reliable yielder is useful only when its fruit suits the grower's purpose.
`产出稳定的品种，只有在其果实符合种植者用途时才有用。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS013 USE:U05005 -->
For a simple harvest comparison, suppose one plot yields twelve kilograms and another yields nine.
`作一个简单的收成比较：假设一块地产出十二千克，另一块产出九千克。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS014 USE:U05006,U01705 -->
The second yield is three kilograms lower; that diminution is a quarter of the first plot's crop, not a quarter of the two crops combined.
`第二块地的产量少三千克；这一减少量是第一块地收成的四分之一，而不是两块地总收成的四分之一。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02aS015 USE:U04605,U04624 -->
Food from the garden can sustain a household, while the roots and their water supply remain essential to the plant's sustenance.
`园圃提供的食物能够维持家庭生活，而根系及其水分供应，仍是植物获得滋养的必要条件。`

# Two ways to change what we harvest

<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS001 USE:U04623 -->
A harvest becomes sustenance only if it reaches the table in usable form.
`收成只有以可食用的形式到达餐桌，才成为维持生命的食物。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS002 USE:U01204 -->
Sugar can conserve fruit by lowering the water available to microorganisms, but sweetness does not replace the rest of a tested preservation process.
`糖可以通过减少微生物可利用的水来保存水果，但甜度不能替代经过检验的保存工序中的其他环节。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS003 USE:U01205,U01206 -->
Pieces kept in a thick syrup give a fruit conserve something to bite into; a fruit-rich conserve spread on toast serves a different texture.
`保存在浓糖浆中的果块，使糖渍水果保留了可咀嚼的口感；抹在吐司上的高果量果酱，则呈现另一种质地。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS004 -->
The National Center for Home Food Preservation's apple conserve combines chopped tart apples, raisins and nuts with lemon juice, pectin and sugar.
`美国家庭食品保存中心的苹果果酱，把切碎的酸苹果、葡萄干和坚果，与柠檬汁、果胶和糖结合起来。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS005 -->
Its directions include both cooking and boiling-water processing, with processing time depending on altitude.
`其操作说明既包括烹煮，也包括沸水处理，而处理时间取决于海拔。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS006 USE:U00804 -->
A jar's capacity tells you its volume, not whether an altered recipe will be safe in it.
`罐子的容量说明的是它的容积，而不能说明改动后的配方装在其中是否安全。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS007 USE:U03202,U02103 -->
In an ordinary dessert served promptly, medium heat might soften apple pieces and cinnamon might act as a flavour enhancer; that is a different task from making a shelf-stable preserve.
`在及时食用的普通甜点中，中火可以使苹果块变软，肉桂可以起增味作用；这与制作能在常温下保存的食品，是不同的任务。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS008 USE:U03204 -->
For a grilled version, keep the fruit over a medium-hot grill and turn it as the surface browns.
`如果改做烤制甜点，可以把水果放在中等热度的烤架上，并在表面变褐时翻动。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS009 USE:U03208 -->
Fermentation offers another transformation: instead of making sugar unavailable, brewers provide yeast with a medium in which it can use sugars.
`发酵提供了另一种转化：酿酒者不是使糖变得不可利用，而是为酵母提供能够利用糖的生长环境。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS010 USE:U01604,U03209 -->
To cultivate the yeast, they need a suitable nutrient medium and controlled conditions, not merely a sealed bottle.
`要培养酵母，需要适合的营养培养基和受控条件，而不仅仅是一只密封瓶。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS011 USE:U02305 -->
ASHRAE's brewing account describes sugar metabolism that evolves carbon dioxide while producing ethanol and releasing heat.
`美国供暖、制冷与空调工程师学会的酿造说明，描述了糖代谢过程中放出二氧化碳、同时产生乙醇并释放热量的过程。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS012 USE:U02311 -->
The evolution of heat is one reason a brewing process has a temperature-control problem as well as a flavour problem.
`热量的释放，是酿造过程除了风味问题之外，还存在温度控制问题的一个原因。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS013 -->
Coopers gives a specific account of natural conditioning: yeast added before packaging consumes residual sugar, and the finished beer is naturally carbonated.
`Coopers对自然熟化提供了一种具体说明：包装前加入的酵母消耗残余糖分，使成品啤酒自然产生碳酸气。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS014 USE:U04401,U04405 -->
Fine sediment of spent yeast settles at the bottom, and gently rolling the bottle redistributes it through the sedimented beer.
`发酵后剩下的酵母形成细小沉渣，沉至底部；轻轻滚动酒瓶，会使沉渣重新分散在已有沉淀的啤酒中。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P02bS015 USE:U02001 -->
When it is poured, the beer emerges from the bottle with a history of living cells, dissolved gas and settling particles behind its appearance.
`倒出时，啤酒从瓶中流出；它的外观背后，包含着活细胞、溶解气体和颗粒沉降的过程。`

# From identifying a species to explaining its past

<!-- SENTENCE:P03S001 USE:U01803 -->
A field botanist needs a feature that discriminates one plant from another before asking how either acquired it.
`野外植物学家在追问两种植物的某个特征如何形成之前，先需要用一个特征把它们区分开来。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S002 USE:U00603 -->
In the Botanical Survey of India's flora of Mahananda, the key to Osbeckia chinensis points to a terminal flower cluster with one to three pairs of approximate leaves immediately below it.
`在印度植物调查局出版的《马哈南达植物志》中，检索表指出：Osbeckia chinensis顶生花簇的紧下方有一至三对彼此近聚的叶片。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S003 -->
The contrasting branch for O. stellata describes an elongated panicle without those clustered leaves beneath it.
`与之相对，O. stellata所在的检索分支描述的是伸长的圆锥花序，其下方没有这样的近聚叶片。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S004 USE:U00602 -->
This gives a useful, approximate description for identification; it does not by itself establish which species descended from which.
`这为鉴定提供了一个有用的大致描述，却不能单凭这一点确定哪个物种由哪个物种演变而来。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P03S005 USE:U02315,U02803 -->
To see the extra step that an evolutionary explanation requires, imagine a population whose young inherit differences in a trait affecting survival.
`要看清进化解释还需要哪一步，可以设想一个种群：其后代会遗传某种影响生存的性状差异。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S006 USE:U02806 -->
A variant passed on as part of the offspring's genetic inheritance can become more frequent across generations if its carriers leave more offspring under particular conditions.
`如果某种变异作为后代遗传特征的一部分传了下来，而在特定条件下，其携带者留下更多后代，这种变异就可能在一代代繁殖中变得更常见。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S007 USE:U02306 -->
The population is evolvable because heritable variation can change in frequency; an individual need not choose to change its body.
`这个种群能够演化，因为可遗传变异的频率能够改变；这并不要求某个个体主动选择改变自己的身体。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S008 USE:U02301,U00104,U00111 -->
Over many generations, a lineage can evolve from land-dwelling ancestors into forms better adapted to life in water; adaptation can arise through such differences in survival and reproduction.
`经过许多代，一个谱系可以从陆生祖先演化为更适于水中生活的形态；这种适应可以通过生存和繁殖方面的差异形成。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P03S009 USE:U02008 -->
Fossils let researchers test accounts of the emergence of whales as fully aquatic mammals.
`化石使研究者能够检验那些解释鲸类如何成为完全水生哺乳动物的论述。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S010 -->
In a 2015 study, Alexandra Houssaye and her colleagues examined the internal structure of bones from three families of early whales, using virtual sections made from scans.
`在一项2015年的研究中，亚历山德拉·乌赛及其同事利用扫描生成的虚拟切片，研究了三个早期鲸类科的骨骼内部结构。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S011 -->
Basilosaurids had flipper-like forelimbs and greatly reduced hindlimbs that were no longer connected to the spine, while earlier groups retained different combinations of land and water locomotion.
`龙王鲸科动物的前肢呈鳍状，后肢已大幅缩小并且不再与脊柱相连；更早的类群则保留着陆地运动与水中运动的不同组合。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S012 USE:U02316 -->
Evolutionarily, the reduced hindlimbs matter because their place in the skeleton can be compared with the much larger, weight-bearing limbs of earlier relatives.
`从进化角度看，缩小的后肢之所以重要，是因为可以把它们在骨架中的位置，与较早近亲大得多、能够承重的肢体进行比较。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S013 USE:U04406 -->
A skeleton recovered from sedimentary rock may constrain a reconstruction's proportions, yet leave its skin colour unknown.
`从沉积岩中发现的骨架可以约束复原作品的身体比例，却仍然无法说明其皮肤颜色。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S014 USE:U04107 -->
To restore an extinct whale in a drawing, an artist must therefore distinguish the bones that survive from the soft tissues that have to be inferred.
`因此，要在图画中推测复原一头灭绝的鲸，画家必须区分现存的骨骼证据与需要推断的软组织。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S015 USE:U04114 -->
A restoration can show those inferences clearly without presenting every painted detail as a discovery.
`一幅复原图可以清楚地展现这些推断，而不必把每一个画出的细节都呈现为已经证实的发现。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S016 -->
The bone study itself cautions against inferring swimming behaviour from bone compactness alone: different parts of the skeleton contribute different evidence.
`这项骨骼研究本身也提醒人们，不能只凭骨骼致密度就推断游泳行为：骨架的不同部分提供不同的证据。`

<!-- SENTENCE:P03S017 USE:U02317 -->
The history of evolutionism also contains arguments about who first explained change through selection.
`用进化观点解释物种起源的思想史中，也存在关于谁最先以选择解释生物变化的争论。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S018 USE:U02318,U00503 -->
In an 1860 letter, Patrick Matthew, an evolutionist who had discussed changing species in his 1831 book on timber, claimed that his account had anticipated Darwin's.
`1860年，进化论支持者帕特里克·马修在一封信中声称，自己1831年林木著作中关于物种变化的论述，早于达尔文的相关解释。`
<!-- SENTENCE:P03S019 USE:U02319 -->
His evolutionistic argument linked excessive reproduction with the loss of less well-suited offspring, although his wider account also included speculations that are not part of modern biology.
