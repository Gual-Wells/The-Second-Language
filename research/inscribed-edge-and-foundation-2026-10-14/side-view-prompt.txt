Each view includes the entire shallow ellipse of the blank upper face, and the front visible side wall. Keep exactly the same coin, thickness, finish, lighting and elevation across panels.

The side wall is a single fine satin silver band bordered by TWO THIN INTEGRAL ROUNDED SHOULDERS, not thick rails. Nominal diameter 40 mm, max rim thickness 3.2 mm. No deep rim grooves, no mechanical teeth, no stacked medallion plates.
