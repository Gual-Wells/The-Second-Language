> The Line on the Map

地图上的那条线

> Here are a river and its forest, a town tested by flood, and courage shown in common need. A line upon a map asks that words be weighed in their setting.

这里有一条河流及其森林，一座经受洪水考验的小镇，以及人们在共同困境中展现的勇气。地图上的一条线提醒我们：应当结合语境，慎重理解词语的含义。

> This leaf presents the chapter’s attestation in specimen form. It awaits the first successful assessment and grants no certification.

此页以样品形式呈现本章的认证文书。它尚待首次测验通过，目前不授予认证。

> May understanding endure beyond this page and find use in speech and writing.

愿这份理解不止于此页，长久留存，并在口语与写作中得到运用。
